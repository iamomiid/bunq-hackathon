# Features

## Smart Budgeting with AI
- The app uses AI to watch your spending.
- It learns from your rules and enforces them automatically.

## Natural-Language Rules
- You can type rules in normal English.
- Example: “Limit groceries to €200 per month.”

## AI Transaction Monitoring
- Every purchase is checked by the AI.
- If a rule is broken, the AI takes action.

## Automatic Card Blocking
- When you reach a limit, your Bunq card stops working.
- This helps you avoid extra spending.

## Flexible Override
- If you need to buy something important, ask the AI.
- The AI may unlock your card for that purchase.

## Demo Dashboard
- See your limits, spending, and remaining budget.
- A simple view helps you track your money.
