# How It Works

## Natural Language Limits
- You type your limits in plain English.  
- Example: “Don’t spend more than €50 on takeaways this week.”  
- The AI reads your words and sets the rule.

## AI Transaction Monitoring
- Every time you pay with your Bunq card, the AI checks the rule.  
- If a transaction breaks a rule, it stops the payment.  
- You can see all checks in the dashboard.

## Flexible Override
- If you need to buy something important after a block, talk to the AI.  
- The AI asks why you need it.  
- If the reason is good, it may unblock your card once.

## Simple Budget Management
1. **Choose Your Budget Card**  
   Pick one Bunq card for budgeting.  
2. **Set Your Rules**  
   Write easy rules in English.  
3. **Track and Control**  
   Watch spending and limits on the dashboard.  
   The AI will help you stay on budget.
